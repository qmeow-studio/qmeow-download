3. The Unicode Character Database
================================================================================

Three dictionaries are built from the Unihan database:

   cangjie.dat   倉頡 and 速成, from kCangjie
   zhuyin.dat    注音, from kMandarin, converted from pinyin to Bopomofo
   reverse.dat   反查, by inverting cangjie.dat and stroke.dat

with kGradeLevel, kHKGlyph, kUnihanCore2020 and kBigFive deciding which
character is offered first. The notice carried by those data files:

    # Unicode Character Database
    # © 2025 Unicode®, Inc.
    # Unicode and the Unicode Logo are registered trademarks of Unicode, Inc.
    # in the U.S. and other countries.
    # For terms of use and license, see https://www.unicode.org/terms_of_use.html

Unicode Version 17.0.0, data files dated 2025-07-24. Generated by
tools/gen_cangjie.py, tools/gen_zhuyin.py and tools/gen_reverse.py; nothing was
transcribed by hand.

7. 教育部《成語典》 (Ministry of Education Dictionary of Chinese Idioms)
================================================================================

   中華民國教育部（Ministry of Education, R.O.C.）。《成語典》
   （版本編號：2020，資料檔 dict_idioms_2020_20260929）
   網址：https://dict.idioms.moe.edu.tw/

The 成語 panel (1.7.207 onward) contains the complete text database of this
dictionary - every field of all 5,489 entries - as assets/idioms/index.txt and
assets/idioms/detail.qmz. It is used under the Creative Commons
Attribution-NoDerivatives 3.0 Taiwan licence (創用CC 姓名標示－禁止改作 3.0
臺灣授權條款):

   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nd/3.0/tw/legalcode

and the Ministry's usage notice (《成語典》公眾授權使用說明), which must be
followed and is published at:

   https://language.moe.gov.tw/001/Upload/Files/site_content/M0001/respub/idiomsdict_10409.pdf

Only the file format was changed (tools/idioms/build_idioms.py): the entry
text - headwords, pronunciations, definitions, sources, citations and usage
notes - is unchanged and is not converted to simplified characters, and
inserted idioms are not passed through the 繁簡 output conversion. The
Excel line-break code "_x000D_" in the source file is stored as a normal
line break.

8. 教育部《重編國語辭典修訂本》 (Revised Mandarin Chinese Dictionary)
================================================================================

   中華民國教育部（Ministry of Education, R.O.C.）。《重編國語辭典修訂本》
   （資料檔 dict_revised_2015_20260929）
   網址：https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/

From 1.7.218, assets/words.dat (the related-word suggestions shown after a
character is typed) also contains words taken from the headword (字詞名)
column of this dictionary - about 149,000 multi-character words. No
definitions or other text of the dictionary are included. Each word is
split into a first character (or first two characters) and the rest, as
the suggestion list needs (tools/words/merge_revised_words.py).

The Ministry publishes this dictionary for public use under its dictionary
public licence (辭典公眾授權); see the 辭典公眾授權網 linked at the foot of
https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/ for the licence and usage notice.

9. English word frequencies and next words (symspellpy data)
================================================================================

From 1.7.219, assets/english.dat uses word frequencies, and gains common
words it lacked, from frequency_dictionary_en_82_765.txt; and
assets/english_next.dat (next-word suggestions) is built from
frequency_bigramdictionary_en_243_342.txt. Both files ship with symspellpy
6.10.2 (https://github.com/mammothb/symspellpy, MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2025 mmb L; original SymSpell Copyright (c) 2021 Wolf Garbe,
https://github.com/wolfgarbe/SymSpell). The counts come from the Google
Books Ngram data (https://books.google.com/ngrams), which "may be freely
used for any purpose", intersected with the SCOWL word lists. Only the
words and their order are used (tools/english/build_english.py).
